# Genshin Roster

A free, fan-made tool for **Genshin Impact**: record the characters you own and their constellations, build teams, and share them as a link or an image. No sign-up, and your data stays in your browser.

**Open it: https://scornae.github.io/genshin-tools/roster/**

## What it does

**Roster**
- Mark which characters you own and set their constellation (C0-C6) and weapon refinement
- Two display modes, with filters by element, weapon, rarity and ownership
- Save your roster as an image with one click
- Export and import your data as a file, so you can back it up or move it to another device

**Team Builder**
- **Create & Share**: build up to 3 teams from any character, then send them as a link, as text, or as an image
- **My Party, Abyss and Stygian**: plan teams from the characters you own
- **Imaginarium Theater**: plan the opening cast, guest stars and supporting cast, with a role for each character

## How to use

1. Open the site and tap a character to mark it as owned and pick its constellation
2. Go to **Team Builder** and press **Add team**
3. Pick a mode. The modes marked "Owned characters only" use the characters you marked in the Roster, so set those first. If you just want to share a team, use **Create & Share**, which lets you pick any character
4. Copy the link, copy the text, or press **Capture** to save the teams as an image

## Privacy

Everything is stored in your own browser. There are no accounts and nothing is sent to a server. Use **Export data** to keep a backup, since clearing your browser data also clears the roster.

## Built with

A single HTML file with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework and no build step.
